## About me

I build practical projects at the intersection of backend development, machine learning and product engineering. Most of my work grows out of real tasks: hackathon prototypes, service backends, applied AI features, automation, and systems that need to go from idea to working demo quickly.

What I enjoy most is taking a problem apart, finding the simplest architecture that can support it, and turning it into a usable product.

## What I work with

- Backend: `C#`, `.NET`, `ASP.NET Core`, `Entity Framework Core`, `MySQL`, `REST API`
- Applied AI and ML: `Python`, `LLM integration`, data processing, experimental ML projects
- Automation and bots: `Telegram bots`, scheduled jobs, service integrations
- Robotics and systems: `ROS2`, sensor data, control logic, low-level integration
- Product prototyping: hackathon MVPs, admin panels, internal tools, web services
